> W: Hello, students. Do you know that plants often help scientists understand the wonders of nature? Let’s look at some plants that are perfect for science experiments because they grow quickly. Second, lettuce grows quickly and is sensitive to environmental conditions. So, scientists grow it to see the effects of various chemicals on plant growth. Third, sunflowers are known for their remarkable growth rate, sometimes reaching several feet in just a few months. That’s why scientists use them to learn about the effects of soils on plant height. Finally, peas are classic plants for experiments about genetics as they grow quickly and have easily observable characteristics like seed color and shape.
