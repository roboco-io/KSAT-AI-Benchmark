26. Dorothy L. Cheney에 관한 다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 <u>않는</u> 것은?

Dorothy L. Cheney, a renowned American biologist, is famous for her research on social relationships of non-human primates like vervet monkeys and baboons. Born in Boston in 1950, she graduated from Wellesley College in 1972. Although she originally planned to go to law school, her career plan changed after she joined her husband, Robert Seyfarth, for fieldwork on animal behavior. Subsequently, she earned a doctoral degree in zoology from Cambridge University. Between 1977 and 1988, she conducted field research in Kenya, discovering that vervet monkeys used different alarm calls for different predators such as leopards and snakes. Afterward, she carried out a project on baboons in Botswana. Cheney and her husband wrote *Baboon Metaphysics*, describing results from their fieldwork. Cheney had a strong influence on researchers of animal behavior, and many graduate students she mentored went on to distinguished careers in animal behavior.

② 로스쿨에 진학할 계획이었다.
③ 케냐에서 현장 연구를 수행했다.
④ *Baboon Metaphysics*를 단독 집필하였다.
⑤ 동물 행동 연구자들에게 큰 영향을 미쳤다.
