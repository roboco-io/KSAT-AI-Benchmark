10. 다음 표를 보면서 대화를 듣고, 남자가 주문할 연을 고르시오.

> W: Honey, what are you doing on your computer?
> M: I’m shopping for a kite for our son Liam.
> W: Do you want me to help you choose one?
> M: Sure. Here are the five most popular models.
> W: Let me see. *[Pause]* I think the 30-inch kite would be too small.
> M: So do I. Then, what about the price?
> W: Their prices seem reasonable except this one. I think 40 dollars is too much for a kite.
> M: I agree. As for the shape, let’s skip the butterfly kite. Liam is not into butterflies.
> W: You’re right. Now we have two options left. One has a kite tail, and the other doesn’t.
> M: Let’s choose the one with a kite tail. It would look cool moving in the wind.
> W: All right. Then, this is the kite for our son.
> M: Great. I’ll order it now.
