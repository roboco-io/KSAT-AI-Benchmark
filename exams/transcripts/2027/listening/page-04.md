4. 대화를 듣고, 그림에서 대화의 내용과 일치하지 <u>않는</u> 것을 고르시오.

> M: Ms. Robinson, what are you looking at?
> W: I’m looking at a picture of the customer waiting room that I’ve just finished remodeling. Do you want to see it?
> M: Sure. *[Pause]* It looks great. I like the two sofas.
> W: I put them there for customers to watch TV while waiting.
> M: Good idea. The star-shaped clock on the wall looks unique.
> W: It does. I bought it from a local artist. What do you think about the stripe-patterned blinds?
> M: I think they make the room cozy.
> W: Yeah. I also put a bookshelf by the window. Customers can read to pass the time.
> M: That’s nice. Look at the teapot on the table. It’s cute.
> W: Thanks. I hope customers like the waiting room.
> M: I’m sure they will.
