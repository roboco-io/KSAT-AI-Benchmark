8. 대화를 듣고, Open Buildings Week에 관해 언급되지 <u>않은</u> 것을 고르시오.

> M: Jane, did you hear about Open Buildings Week? Our city is opening some historic and well-known buildings to the public.
> W: Interesting! When is it?
> M: The event runs from December 14th to December 20th. But we need to register in advance.
> W: Okay. How can I register for it?
> M: You need to fill out and submit a registration form on the city website.
> W: All right. Is there a participation fee?
> M: Yes. For 2 dollars, anyone can explore the buildings. I have more information on my phone. Take a look.
> W: Oh, there’s a special lecture about our city’s architectural history, too.
> M: Isn’t that fantastic? How about going to the event together?
> W: Sounds great.
