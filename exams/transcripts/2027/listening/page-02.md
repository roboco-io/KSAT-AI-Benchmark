2. 대화를 듣고, 남자의 의견으로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

> W: Grandpa, the news said lots of people went to the fireworks festival yesterday. I think fireworks are so amazing.
> M: I know fireworks are pretty, but I’m not a fan of them.
> W: Why not? Everybody likes fireworks.
> M: Well, fireworks can be harmful to the environment.
> W: What do you mean?
> M: When fireworks explode, they can leave toxic chemicals behind, which might pollute the air, soil, and water.
> W: That’s surprising. I’ve never thought they could hurt the environment.
> M: Also, fireworks make big booming sounds and very bright flashes. This can panic birds nearby.
> W: Oh, that could make them fly away from their nests, right?
> M: Exactly. Like I said, fireworks can harm the environment.
> W: Thank you for letting me know.
