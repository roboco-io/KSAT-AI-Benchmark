14. 대화를 듣고, 남자의 마지막 말에 대한 여자의 응답으로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오. [3점]

> W: Dad, this restaurant is amazing.
> M: It is. Are you ready to order?
> W: Well, I’m thinking about the shrimp pasta. *[Pause]* Look, Dad! Isn’t that Mariah Windey sitting over there?
> M: Oh, you’re right! She was in your favorite movie, *The Queen and Love*.
> W: I’d love to get a photo with her. Let’s go over there now.
> M: I think we’d better not.
> W: Why not? I don’t want to miss this opportunity.
> M: It seems that she’s having dinner with her family, just like us. We should respect her privacy.
> W: Yeah, that’s probably true. But a photo takes only a minute. Wouldn’t that be okay?
> M: It’s not about how long it takes. You would be interrupting her meal. So, we should leave her alone.
