5. 대화를 듣고, 여자가 할 일로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

> M: Clara, our school band club is volunteering at the community center for seniors tomorrow.
> W: I’m excited to play music for them. As club leaders, let’s double-check everything’s ready.
> M: Okay. I tested the sound equipment this morning. It all works.
> W: Great. For the group singing, I printed out the words for the songs.
> M: That’s good. What about the tambourines?
> W: I packed them. The seniors will enjoy shaking them while singing.
> M: They will. And the center will provide snacks for the seniors, so we don’t have to buy any snacks.
> W: Excellent. Do we need to check anything else?
> M: We need to get our club T-shirts from the school lobby. They were delivered earlier today.
> W: No worries. I’ll do that.
> M: Thanks! Then, everything’s set.
