6. 대화를 듣고, 남자가 지불할 금액을 고르시오.

> W: Welcome to the Grand Circus Museum. How may I help you?
> M: Hi, I’d like to purchase admission tickets. How much are they?
> W: They’re 10 dollars per person. The ticket price is the same for both children and adults.
> M: That’s fine. I need five tickets for my family.
> W: Sure. For your information, there’s a special exhibition on the history of Asian circuses. It costs 4 dollars per person to experience it.
> M: Sounds interesting. I’ll take five special exhibition tickets as well.
> W: Okay. So, you want five admission tickets and five special exhibition tickets, right?
> M: Correct. Can I use this discount coupon?
> W: Let me see. *[Pause]* Yes, you get a 10 percent discount off your total purchase.
> M: Great. Here’s my credit card.
