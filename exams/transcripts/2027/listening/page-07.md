7. 대화를 듣고, 여자가 K-music Pop-up Market에 갈 수 <u>없는</u> 이유를 고르시오.

> M: Hi, Maya. Where are you heading?
> W: I’m on the way to the library to return some books.
> M: Okay. Guess what? The K-music Pop-up Market will open this Saturday for one day only. Let’s go together.
> W: I’m afraid I can’t go.
> M: Really? Are you still working part-time on weekends?
> W: No, I don’t work weekends anymore.
> M: Well, I heard you have a debate competition next week. Are you preparing for it this Saturday?
> W: I’m not. The competition has been delayed until next month.
> M: Then, why can’t you go to the pop-up market?
> W: Actually, I’m going camping with my family this weekend.
> M: Oh, I see. I hope you have fun with your family.
